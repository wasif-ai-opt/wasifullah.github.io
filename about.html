About

I am a PhD Researcher at the James Watt School of Engineering, University of Glasgow, where I work on self-learning composite dispatching rules for scheduling in dynamic manufacturing systems. My research sits at the intersection of manufacturing systems, computational optimization and computational intelligence, with the aim of making production scheduling more adaptive, interpretable and useful in practice.

Before starting my doctorate, I completed an MSc in Manufacturing Systems at Universiti Malaysia Pahang, Malaysia, where I also gained teaching assistant experience, and a BEng in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Engineering and Technology, Peshawar, Pakistan. My earlier experience includes industrial training, which shaped my interest in the gap between scheduling theory and the realities of the shop floor.
