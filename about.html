About

Wasif Ullah.

I am a PhD Researcher at the James Watt School of Engineering, University of Glasgow, working on self-learning composite dispatching rules for scheduling in dynamic manufacturing systems. My research sits at the intersection of manufacturing systems, evolutionary computation and computational intelligence, with the aim of making production scheduling more adaptive, interpretable and useful in practice.

Before starting my doctorate, I completed a master’s degree in Manufacturing Systems at Universiti Malaysia Pahang Al-Sultan Abdullah, Malaysia, and a bachelor’s degree in Mechanical Engineering at the University of Engineering and Technology, Peshawar, Pakistan. My earlier experience — as a CNC programmer, a production trainee and a visiting research fellow — shaped my interest in the gap between scheduling theory and the realities of the shop floor.
