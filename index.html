Ongoing PhD project

Self-learning composite dispatching rules

University of Glasgow · since 2025

An evolutionary hyper-heuristic framework that automatically designs composite dispatching rules from shop-floor attributes, learning from production data and staying robust under dynamic conditions such as machine breakdowns, rush orders and uncertain processing times — while keeping the evolved rules interpretable for practitioners.

  • Research overview
2025 Journal · IF 7.5

Greedy-assisted teaching-learning-based optimization for cost-based hybrid flow shop scheduling

Wasif Ullah, Mohd Fadzil Faisae Ab Rashid, Muhammad Ammar Nik Mu’tasim

Expert Systems with Applications, 273, 126955

A greedy-assisted TLBO algorithm that minimizes total production cost in hybrid flow shop scheduling, demonstrating how learning-inspired metaheuristics can be strengthened with constructive heuristics for cost-oriented industrial scheduling.

2026 Under review

From dispatching rules to self-learning scheduling

Wasif Ullah, Peifeng Li

Swarm and Evolutionary Computation · submitted

A critical review tracing the transition from classical, hand-designed dispatching rules to self-learning scheduling, synthesising evolutionary and learning-based approaches and identifying open problems for intelligent manufacturing.

Wasif Ullah, Muhammad Ammar Nik Mu’tasim, Mohd Fadzil Faisae Ab Rashid

International Journal of Automotive and Mechanical Engineering, 21(3), 11616–11628

Applies the TLBO metaheuristic to cost-oriented hybrid flow shop scheduling, analysing solution quality and computational behaviour against established approaches.

About

Background

I am a PhD Researcher at the James Watt School of Engineering, University of Glasgow, working with Peifeng Li on self-learning dispatching rules for dynamic manufacturing scheduling. I received my master’s degree in Manufacturing Systems from Universiti Malaysia Pahang Al-Sultan Abdullah in 2025 — where my thesis on cost-oriented hybrid flow shop scheduling received a Best Thesis Award — and my bachelor’s degree in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Engineering and Technology, Peshawar, in 2022. Earlier in my career I worked as a CNC programmer and as a visiting research fellow at NPUST, Taiwan.
