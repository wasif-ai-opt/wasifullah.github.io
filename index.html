Wasif Ullah

PhD Researcher James Watt School of Engineering, University of Glasgow Glasgow, United Kingdom

Hello, I am Wasif Ullah, a doctoral researcher in manufacturing systems engineering. My PhD research, Self-Learning Composite Dispatching Rules for Scheduling in Dynamic Manufacturing Systems, investigates how evolutionary computation and machine learning can be used to automatically design scheduling heuristics that adapt to the changing conditions of real production environments.

More broadly, I am interested in how computational intelligence can support decision-making on the factory floor, from classical dispatching rules and metaheuristics to emerging learning-based and language-model-driven approaches.

Research interests

  • Manufacturing scheduling
  • Computational optimization
  • Evolutionary computation
  • Computational intelligence
